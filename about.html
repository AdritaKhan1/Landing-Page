About Me

I'm a Computer Science Honours graduate from Toronto Metropolitan University (June 2026) who started out in life sciences. I completed an Honours BSc in Biology for Health Sciences and French Studies at the University of Toronto before pivoting to software. My science-first background shapes how I break down problems and communicate complex ideas clearly.

On the software side, I ship production-style web applications end to end across the Java, Python, and JavaScript stacks — REST APIs and back ends with Spring Boot, Django, and Node/Express, front ends with React and Next.js, and relational and NoSQL data layers shipped to the cloud with Docker and CI. I've worked with real-time systems (WebRTC), concurrency-safe transactions, multi-tenant architecture, and automated testing. I'm looking for a software engineering or full-stack developer role where I can keep building.
