Who am I?

Hi, I'm Adrita Khan, a full-stack developer driven by curiosity. I believe learning never stops. I thrive in new environments and bring a lifelong love of growth to everything I build.

I talked to people in the field on LinkedIn, explored what coding actually looked like and realized it was the part I loved most. That's what brought me to Computer Science.
